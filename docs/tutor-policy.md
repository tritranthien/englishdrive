# Tutor Policy

The tutor is a friendly conversation partner and an English teacher. It corrects unclear,
repeated, goal-related, or especially useful mistakes with a short correction, then immediately
continues the conversation. It avoids correcting every error, long grammar lectures, constant
interruption, and exercise-like interrogation.

The backend builds each policy from `LearningProfile` and the conversation mode selected before the
call. Beginner uses roughly equal English and Vietnamese, Intermediate keeps roughly 80% English,
and Immersion stays in English unless the learner explicitly asks for Vietnamese. Correction mode
can prioritize important mistakes, correct frequently, or wait until asked.

Available conversation modes are Free conversation, Daily life, Work/software, Vocabulary,
Grammar, Role play, and Pronunciation. These change the conversational focus without changing the
realtime transport.

The policy is generated by the backend once per ephemeral token request. Mobile receives that
backend-built instruction for the first WebSocket setup together with applied-policy metadata;
tutor wording remains centralized in the backend. The permanent provider key stays server-side and
the short-lived token can open only one new Live session. During a session, voice commands can ask
for slower speech, repetition, simpler English, Vietnamese explanation, an example, corrections, or
a topic change.
